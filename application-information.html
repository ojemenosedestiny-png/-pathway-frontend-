About your application details

Creating an applicant account provides your email address and a password. You can then add your contact and application details.

Why these details are requested

Your details support your applicant account, email verification and the school's review of your application. Account messages are sent to the email address you provide. Keep your password and verification links private.

Before you submit

The school's complete privacy policy, including retention periods and contact arrangements for data requests, is awaiting approval. This information page does not replace that policy. If you need these details before sharing personal information, contact the school before creating an account.
