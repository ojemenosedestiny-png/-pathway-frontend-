### Cambridge name and logo findings

Current guidance restricts customer logos to registered schools/partners and requires written approval of materials, with Associate approval where applicable. The school's own identity must remain primary and independent. The older logo PDF also prohibits putting the shield into an organisation's name/logo and warns against adding Cambridge to an organisation name. These rules do not grant this school permission. Use plain programme descriptions without an approval or affiliation statement. [C11], [C12].

Conflict: the older PDF mentions letterhead use; current web guidance limits logos to marketing materials. Do not resolve this by selecting the permissive version: keep the logo removed and request current written guidance from Cambridge. No trademark-law opinion is offered here.

## Storage and publication

`backend/prisma/exam-research-draft.json` contains draft references, never school prices. `npm run seed:exam-research` previews without connecting to the database; `-- --apply` inserts draft settings without overwriting owner edits. This has not been applied to production. Site content ? draft research shows the rows; programme controls import one as Draft. Publishing requires amount/currency/session/date/source and confidence HIGH. References show Needs re-check after 90 days or a changed session. Invoices still use only the owner-set Exam fee, not research data. Refund policy remains Draft and has no public endpoint.
