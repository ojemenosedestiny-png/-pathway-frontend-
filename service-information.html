Before you apply or pay

NewworldPathway provides exam-preparation and study-abroad guidance through New World Emerald Private School. It does not award university degrees.

Preparation and official registration

This is a preparation enquiry. Official exam registration is confirmed separately.

Ask the school to confirm the programme, subjects, teaching dates, venue and what a quoted charge covers. Preparation, official examination entry, university applications and accommodation are different services. A directory listing or external link does not confirm an affiliation, available place or admission.

Confirm the fee in writing

View published school fees or request an itemised quote before paying. Ask whether the amount includes official examination entry or any third-party charge. Check the currency, payment recipient, deadline and refund terms for the specific service.

Ask about changes or refunds

Contact the school with your payment reference and the service concerned. Ask for the applicable written refund terms before making a payment. This page does not set a refund entitlement or cancellation deadline.

Applying does not guarantee admission

The relevant examination body or university determines its own eligibility requirements, official registration and admission decisions.
